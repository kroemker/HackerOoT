Where HackerOoT's 8 MB of RAM actually goes

Vanilla Ocarina of Time capped its usable RAM around 4.3 MB (0x8044BE80). HackerOoT raises SYS_CFB_END to 0x80800000 — a flat 8 MB — which is also why there's real headroom for something like a persistent ocean scene with per-island collision.

1. The full address space

Three regions, low address to high. Only the framebuffers and the PlayState arena have exact, config-driven sizes — the boot/code region is whatever the linked ROM produces.

2. Inside the PlayState arena

When gameplay starts, one THA_AllocTailAlign16 call carves PLAY_ALLOC_SIZE (0x4D4790 ≈ 4.83 MB) out of the System Heap. Everything below is sub-allocated from that one block.
